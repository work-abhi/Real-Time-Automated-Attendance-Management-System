FAQ
Common questions
Quick answers for setup, accuracy, and workflow.
Is face recognition accurate?

Yes—encodings are scoped to your organization. The system uses tolerance-based matching and logs each verification step.

How does GPS attendance work?

Office coordinates and a configurable radius are stored. Check-in succeeds only if the device location is within the allowed area.

What about WiFi verification?

It validates that the device is on the configured network (public IP match) before allowing attendance. This reduces spoofing risk.

Do you support OTP/QR?

Yes. OTP/QR provide backup workflows during camera failures or special scenarios—while keeping verification traceability.
